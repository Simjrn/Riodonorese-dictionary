### Notes

##### Star

Both 'stou' and 'stás' are forms of the verb 'star', which means 'to be'. However, you already learnt a different way to say 'I am' and 'you are' in the last lesson, so what's the difference? Here are the uses of both:

- *sou* and *yes* are used for general statements, such as lining nouns to permanent adjectives and other nouns, such as names
- *stou* and *stás* are used for temporary states of being, such as emotions and where you are

##### Obrigado and obrigada

Just like in Portuguese, you use different ways to say 'thank you' depending on whether you are a man or a woman. Remember to pronounce them like 'Obrigáu' or 'Obrigáa'.
